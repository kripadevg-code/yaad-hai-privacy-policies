## Account and Data Deletion — YaadHai

Users of **YaadHai** (`com.yaadhai`) can request the deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

### How to request account deletion:
1. Send an email to **support@yaadhai.com** (or your registered support email).
2. Use the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request - YaadHai"**.
3. Send the email from the Google account you used to sign in to the app.

### What data will be deleted:
* **Account details:** Your user profile, email address, and authentication record in Supabase Auth will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
* **Device / Local data:** All your study material, flashcards, subjects, and quizzes are stored locally on your device (Drift/SQLite). You can delete them at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

### What data is kept:
* No personal study data or images are retained on our servers.
