## Account and Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users can easily request deletion of their account and personal data.

### Steps to Request Account Deletion:
1. Send an email to **ayushishikshaofficial@gmail.com**
2. Use the subject line: **`Account Deletion Request - YaadHai`**
3. Send the email from the Google account registered with the app.

### What Happens Next:
* **Account Credentials:** Your Supabase authentication record (User ID, Email, Profile details) will be permanently purged within **7 business days**.
* **Study Content:** All your subjects, chapters, flashcards, and quizzes are stored locally on your own device (SQLite/Drift). You can delete all local data anytime by clearing app data in your phone settings or uninstalling the app.
